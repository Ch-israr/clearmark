About ClearMark

ClearMark exists because AI-generated work is now routinely flagged by the files themselves — not by what’s in them, but by invisible markers baked into them.

When an AI tool creates an image, a video or an audio clip, it can embed markers: Google’s SynthID signal in the pixels or waveform, and a C2PA / Content Credentials record describing the tool that made it. Platforms read those markers and label, limit or reject the file — even when the work is yours and you have every right to share it.

ClearMark removes the markers it can remove completely — C2PA records, EXIF, XMP and ID3 metadata — and applies a best-effort disruption pass to the SynthID signal. Every cleaned file is re-checked before it is offered for download, and failed jobs are never charged.

We are deliberately honest about the limit: SynthID removal cannot be guaranteed by anyone, because the detector is not public. We would rather tell you that plainly than sell you a promise we cannot keep.

Files are uploaded to our server for cleaning and deleted within an hour. Nothing is kept, shared or used for training.
