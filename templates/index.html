    What is SynthID?

    SynthID is Google’s system for marking AI-generated content. It embeds a signal directly into the pixels of an image, the frames of a video, or the waveform of audio — invisible to the eye, but readable by detectors. When you post a SynthID-marked file, platforms can identify it as AI-generated and label it, limit its reach, or reject it.

    Removing that signal is a cat-and-mouse problem: the signal is designed to survive cropping, resizing and recompression. ClearMark attacks it with pixel-level perturbation and frequency-aware adjustments — the same class of technique other tools describe — and re-encodes the file. That makes the signal much harder to read. It is best-effort by nature, and any service that promises a guarantee is not being straight with you.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is SynthID removal guaranteed?

    No. SynthID disruption is best-effort. Google does not publish its detector, so no one can verify a signal is gone — including us, and including any competitor that claims a guarantee. What we can guarantee is that C2PA, EXIF and XMP metadata are removed and re-checked before you download.
