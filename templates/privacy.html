Privacy Policy

ClearMark processes your files on our server, then deletes them. This page explains exactly what we keep and what we don’t.

Files you upload

Files are uploaded to our server so they can be cleaned. Uploads and cleaned results are stored temporarily and deleted within one hour of upload. We do not keep copies, share your files with anyone, or use them to train models.

Your account

We store your email address and a hashed password (never the password itself), plus your credit balance and a record of your cleaning jobs: file name, media type, status and credit cost. Job records are kept so you can see your recent removals and so failed jobs can be refunded.

What we do not collect

We do not run analytics that follow you across the web, and we do not sell account or usage data. The site does not use third-party tracking cookies.

Payments

Payments are not live in this version, so no payment details are collected. When paid plans open, payments will be handled by a payment provider and we will update this page first.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us.
